What is a maternity care desert?

A county with no hospital or birth center offering obstetric care, and no obstetric clinicians (OB-GYNs, certified nurse midwives, or family doctors who deliver babies).

March of Dimes sorts every county into one of four levels:

  • Maternity care desert: no birthing facility and no obstetric clinicians
  • Low access: fewer than 2 birthing facilities and fewer than 60 obstetric clinicians per 10,000 births, with 10%+ of women uninsured
  • Moderate access: the same, but under 10% of women uninsured
  • Full access: 2+ birthing facilities, or 60+ obstetric clinicians per 10,000 births

These labels describe what's inside a county line. The drive time shows how far the nearest birthing hospital really is. Rural = USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes 7 to 9.
